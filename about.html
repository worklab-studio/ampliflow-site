01 / Origin
Deepak Yadav Founder · Ampliflow

I've watched founders, SDRs, and recruiters pour money into LinkedIn outreach tools charging $79/mo for what should cost half. So we built Ampliflow — every feature you'd actually use, none of the bloat, at the price it should be.

I spent three years running outbound at two startups. Every tool I touched was either overpriced, over-engineered, or shipping features nobody used while missing the ones that mattered. Pricing felt arbitrary, dashboards were noise, and the deliverability story was always "don't worry about it."

Ampliflow is what I wish had existed on day one. Founding members lock in $19/mo for life. That's how we keep it honest as we grow.

02 / Mission

Three things we refuse to compromise.

Every roadmap call, every pricing change, every support reply runs through these three filters first.

Half the price.

If a feature ships in Dripify or Expandi, we ship it for half. Pricing should reflect what software costs to run, not what the market will bear.

Same safety.

Real-time risk scoring, daily caps, anomaly detection. Cutting the price doesn't mean cutting the guardrails.

Real human support.

Every founding member gets a private Slack with the people who write the code. No tickets, no chat bots.
